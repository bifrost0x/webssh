radar

Find SSH hosts

Looks for SSH and SFTP servers in a network you choose. Nothing is saved until you add a host.

Up to 256 addresses, for example a /24. Up to three ports, separated by commas.

What the scan does
  • Opens one TCP connection per address and port, at most about three per second, and only reads the greeting an SSH server sends by itself.
  • It never logs in, sends no credentials and stores nothing.
  • Addresses that SSH connections are not allowed to reach are skipped.
  • The scan is not hidden. Firewalls or intrusion detection on your network may still record it. Only scan networks you are responsible for.
